Highlights

- Human review of 491 records validated screening; 115 studies were included
- Most studies (82.6%) use time, but half rely on fixed, non-learned operations
- Transformer or state-space temporal models appear in 8.7% of studies, all since 2023
- Cardiac and neurologic applications account for nearly 90% of included studies
- External validation (16.5%) and data release (2.6%) are rare; twelve gaps identified

Highlights

- Human review of 491 records validated screening; 120 studies were included
- Most studies (80.8%) use time, but half rely on fixed, non-learned operations
- Transformer or state-space temporal models appear in 8.3% of studies, all since 2023
- Cardiac and neurologic applications account for nearly 90% of included studies
- External validation (16%) and data release (2.5%) are rare; twelve gaps identified

\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
\item \edit{Human review of 491 records validated screening; 115 studies were included}
\item \edit{Most studies (82.6\%) use temporal data, but half rely on fixed operations}
\item \edit{Transformer or state-space temporal models appear in 8.7\% of studies, all since 2023}
\item Cardiac and neurologic applications account for nearly 90\% of included studies
\item \edit{External validation (16.5\%) and data release (2.6\%) are rare; twelve gaps identified}
\end{itemize}
